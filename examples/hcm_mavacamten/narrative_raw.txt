Bristol-Myers Squibb sponsors multiple interventional and observational studies across various phases and settings, including NCT04349072, NCT05582395, NCT05719805, NCT06372457, and NCT07361289 [NCT04349072, NCT05582395, NCT05719805, NCT06372457, NCT07361289]. Phase 3 interventional trials with posted results include NCT03470545, NCT05174416, NCT04349072, and NCT05414175, all of which had actual enrollments ranging from 38 to 251 participants [NCT03470545, NCT05174416, NCT04349072, NCT05414175]. Phase 1 studies include NCT05135871 with 45 actual participants, NCT05719805 with 84 actual participants, and NCT05362045 with 158 actual participants [NCT05135871, NCT05719805, NCT05362045]. Several observational studies feature large estimated enrollments, such as NCT05489705 with an estimated 1700 participants and NCT07846059 with an estimated 800 participants [NCT05489705, NCT07846059]. Additionally, NCT07695272 reported an actual enrollment of 2000 participants in an observational setting [NCT07695272]. Studies such as NCT07155434 and NCT05582395 have a status of TERMINATED [NCT07155434, NCT05582395].
